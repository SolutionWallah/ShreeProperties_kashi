# Shree Property Kashi — Public Home and Property Discovery

## World

Varanasi's riverside architecture is the identifying setting for a warm, editorial property service. The supplied screens set the identity: ivory paper, deep green, vermilion orange, a temple mark, Hindi brand line, elegant serif titles, and crisp sans-serif detail. The concept-seed assignment was grounded direction 3; its network roll was unavailable and no challengers were returned. The user-supplied screen references pin this visual world.

## First viewport

The masthead carries the temple mark, brand, Hindi line, page navigation, phone, and WhatsApp actions. Below it, a broad riverside hero puts the destination itself in view, with the headline and direct actions anchored at left. A five-part trust row leads directly into the featured Varanasi listings.

## Visitor path

Understand the local service and contact it, or use the property filters to find a relevant listing. The home page presents three featured examples; the full `/properties` route supports transaction, category, locality, price, search, and sort controls.

## Signature interaction

The home page's category pills update the featured listings in place. The properties route combines searchable results with an apply/reset filter panel and price sorting.

## Cross-surface reach

Reuse the header, footer, property cards, icon set, token values, and sample property data across the future public detail pages and admin listing tools. The riverside footer illustration is an independent image element at the end of the footer, never a page background.

## Honest risk

The supplied references contain example property details and unsupported business claims. Keep the example data visibly labeled and leave business metrics out until verified.
