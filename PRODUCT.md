# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Provisional audience, inferred from the supplied references:** people looking to buy, rent, or invest in residential and commercial property in Varanasi.
- **Provisional secondary audience, inferred from the admin references:** the property team that publishes and manages listings.

## Product Purpose

- **Provisional, inferred from the references:** Shree Property Kashi presents local property listings and helps visitors contact a Varanasi real estate consultant.
- The public site should make it easy to discover suitable properties and start a direct conversation.

## Positioning

The supplied screens position the service around local Varanasi property knowledge and direct consultant support. This positioning is based on reference content and should be confirmed before being treated as published factual claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The requested first slice is the public home and property discovery experience.
- Property management and the add-property form are later slices shown in the references.
- The existing repository is a Next.js application; retain that stack.
- The implementation should use reusable components, a shared token and theme system, and concise one-line function comments.
- The footer riverside illustration is a distinct image at the bottom of the footer, not a page background.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Shree Property Kashi.
- Preserve the supplied Varanasi identity: warm ivory surfaces, deep green, orange accents, editorial serif display type, the Kashi riverside illustration, and the Hindi brand line “आप का विश्वास ही, मेरी पहचान हैं”.
- Reference screens are the source for the initial visual direction.

## Evidence on Hand

- Nine user-supplied JPEG reference screens are available in the request; they show a public home page, property discovery, property details, an about page, footer, and two admin workflows.
- Listing details shown in the screens are example content. Do not present invented business metrics, client counts, or experience claims as verified facts.

## Product Principles

- Make Varanasi property discovery clear and direct.
- Show useful listing information before asking visitors to contact the consultant.
- Preserve the local identity across public pages and management tools.
- Keep content and layout maintainable through shared components and tokens.
